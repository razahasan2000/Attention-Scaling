## Additional candidates found (all VERIFIED via APIs)

1. **Learning When to Concentrate or Divert Attention: Self-Adaptive Attention Temperature for Neural Machine Translation** — Junyang Lin, Xu Sun, Xuancheng Ren, Muyu Li, Qi Su. **EMNLP 2018** (Brussels), pp. 2985–2990. DOI 10.18653/v1/D18-1331; arXiv:1808.07374. Source: ACL Anthology https://aclanthology.org/D18-1331/ (abstract: proposes Self-Adaptive Control of Temperature (SACT), an attention "temperature" that makes attention softer for some token types and sharper for others). Directly on-topic: learned per-position attention softmax temperature.

2. **Attention Temperature Matters in Abstractive Summarization Distillation** — Shengqiang Zhang, Xingxing Zhang, Hangbo Bao, Furu Wei. **ACL 2022 (Long Papers)**, Dublin, pp. 127–141. DOI 10.18653/v1/2022.acl-long.11; arXiv:2106.03441. Source: ACL Anthology https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.11/ (abstract: manipulating attention temperatures in Transformers makes pseudo-labels easier for student models in distillation). Directly on-topic: attention temperature as a tunable logit-scale knob.

3. **Stabilizing Transformer Training by Preventing Attention Entropy Collapse** — Shuangfei Zhai, Tatiana Likhomanenko, Etai Littwin, Dan Busbridge, Jason Ramapuram, Yizhe Zhang, Jiatao Gu, Joshua M. Susskind. **ICML 2023 (40th ICML)**, PMLR **202:40770–40803**. arXiv:2303.06296. Sources: PMLR v202 index https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ (entry zhai23a; authors + pages) and arXiv abs https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06296 (abstract: proposes Σ-Reparameterization to prevent softmax attention-entropy collapse and improve training stability). Directly on-topic: attention sharpness/entropy and optimization stability under logit scaling.

4. **Variance Sensitivity Induces Attention Entropy Collapse and Instability in Transformers** — Jonghyun Hong, Sungyoon Lee. **EMNLP 2025 (Main)**, Suzhou, pp. 8360–8378. DOI 10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.421. Source: ACL Anthology https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.421/ (abstract: identifies softmax's high variance sensitivity as a cause of attention entropy collapse; entropy-stable attention prevents it and stabilizes training; attention concentration increases the probability-matrix norm → gradient explosion). On-topic: theory of why logit variance (scaling) affects softmax sharpness and training stability. *Note: OpenAlex lists pages 8371–8389; the ACL Anthology record (8360–8378) is authoritative for ACL publications — I use the Anthology value.*

5. **On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks** — Chuan Guo, Geoff Pleiss, Yu Sun, Kilian Q. Weinberger. **ICML 2017 (34th ICML)**, PMLR **70:1321–1330**. arXiv:1706.04599. Sources: PMLR v70 index https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/ (entry guo17a; pages 1321-1330); existence + authors via OpenAlex search. Canonical origin of *temperature scaling* (T) to recalibrate confidence — the standard checkpoint for "temperature" in softmax-based outputs, before its attention-logit generalization.
